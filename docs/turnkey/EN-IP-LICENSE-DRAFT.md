# Draft IP Assignment and License Terms — Caffio Coffee

> **Working draft, not legal advice or an executed agreement.** Qualified counsel in the parties’ agreed jurisdiction must review it and complete party details, price, tax, warranties, and governing law before signature.

## 1. Parties and purpose

The seller and buyer, as identified in a final agreement, may transfer or license the digital assets listed in the approved delivery schedule. The purpose is to permit the buyer to use the Caffio Coffee project within the agreed scope, not to transfer platform accounts, vendor services, or third-party rights that the seller does not own or cannot transfer.

## 2. Included assets

Included assets are only those expressly listed in the delivery appendix: application source, non-secret configuration, documentation, schema records, transferable visual assets, and a domain where registrar and party approvals exist. Excluded assets include API keys, hosting/OAuth accounts, customer data, payment records, non-transferable licenses, and any item excluded by the manifest.

## 3. Right granted

The parties must select in writing either: (a) a non-exclusive transferable/non-transferable license, or (b) an exclusive assignment of rights capable of assignment. Exclusivity, white-label rights, and trademark transfer are never assumed. The final agreement must define territory, term, modification rights, resale rights, and treatment of later improvements.

## 4. Seller and buyer obligations

The seller will deliver the agreed checklist items and remove personal secrets from the bundle. The buyer is responsible for security, compliance, privacy, third-party-license review, provider accounts, configuration, operations, and policies. Delivery does not promise a live store, revenue, performance, or third-party-platform acceptance.

## 5. Trademarks, content, and external assets

The buyer must not use any logo, name, photography, font, or copy unless the appendix proves transfer or an adequate license. Every asset not validly resale/assignment-capable must be replaced or separately licensed. No right to use “Caffio” or a similar mark transfers unless expressly stated.

## 6. Acceptance and support

The final agreement may provide a defined technical acceptance window for installation and agreed validation. Post-acceptance support, hours, limits, price, service level, and exclusions belong in a separate schedule. Support does not include live payments, fulfillment, compliance, or marketing unless expressly purchased.

## 7. Warranties and limitations

Any representation is limited to delivery of the files identified in the manifest on the handover date. This project does not warrant absence of vulnerabilities, uninterrupted service, fitness for a particular purpose, revenue, or perpetual compatibility with APIs, platforms, or browsers. Liability, indemnity, governing law, and dispute resolution require counsel-approved final terms.

## 8. Signature and required schedules

Transfer completes only after identified parties sign, agreed consideration is paid, and schedules cover the asset list, exclusions, license status, domain/DNS steps, secret-removal confirmation, support plan, and buyer acknowledgement of simulation, security, and data boundaries.
