# Acquisition Video Script — English

**Target duration:** 90 seconds. **Audience:** digital-asset buyer, coffee brand, or commercial studio. **Principle:** do not represent a live store, revenue, security certification, or verified tasting data where evidence does not exist.

| Time | Scene and motion | On-screen copy | English voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00–00:10 | Calm Hero reveal: Caffio mark, hero image, brief light-to-dark transition | **CAFFIO COFFEE** / Bilingual editorial coffee experience | “Not every coffee site is an acquisition-ready asset. Caffio brings brand, interaction, and content governance into one extensible experience.” |
| 00:10–00:18 | Switch to Arabic and RTL, then reveal header/navigation behavior | Arabic / English / RTL + LTR | “One interface preserves meaning across Arabic and English, with a considered type system, routes, and dark mode.” |
| 00:18–00:32 | Show collection, search/filtering, then coffee-selection assistant | Search, compare, record-guided selection | “Instead of opaque recommendations, the selection assistant starts with listed brew methods and roast profiles, then explains the match.” |
| 00:32–00:43 | Step through interactive brew guide; ratio, heat, grind, and timing | Interactive brew guide / practical starting points | “Field-note recipes become an interactive guide: repeatable steps with room to adapt the cup.” |
| 00:43–00:55 | Compare two records, share URL, show Open Graph/PDF comparison | Shareable comparison / server-rendered previews | “Comparison supports two records, sharing, and export, while the server layer protects product facts from inference.” |
| 00:55–01:06 | Tasting reflection flow, then administrator moderation states | Review before publication / governed UGC | “Every reflection is moderated. Only approved customer input reaches the public view, separate from batch facts.” |
| 01:06–01:18 | Cart, Society, tracking, and payment boundary states | Local simulation only / no live charge or shipment | “Commerce, subscription, and tracking journeys are available for product demonstration, never misrepresented as payment, shipment, or customer records.” |
| 01:18–01:26 | Source tree, tests, documentation, and sales-bundle material | React + TypeScript + Express + tRPC + Drizzle | “The bundle combines structured source, handover documentation, sharing infrastructure, and a clear diligence path for an acquiring team.” |
| 01:26–01:30 | Caffio mark on charcoal with restrained brass line motion | **Acquire the foundation. Build the operation.** | “Acquire the foundation, then launch it with your providers, policies, and verified data.” |

## Production direction

Use short opacity/transform transitions under 300ms, licensed or original music only, and phone-readable typography. Keep a “local simulation” label visible whenever commerce or payment appears, and a “batch document pending” label near unsupported product data. Do not introduce market-size, revenue, or security-guarantee claims unless independently sourced and approved in a later revision.
