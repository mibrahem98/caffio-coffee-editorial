# MIZAN COFFEE — Brand Direction

## Rebrand iteration

The project now presents **MIZAN COFFEE / ميزان** as a specialty coffee house rather than a static APEX ROAST brand manual. The name is chosen for its association with balance, calibration, and considered craft. It gives the coffee brand a concise Arabic-rooted identity that remains legible and premium in English-facing contexts.

## Chosen direction: Quiet Roast Editorial

MIZAN keeps the original **Quiet Summit Editorial** foundation, then makes it more useful as a public-facing coffee experience. The visual language combines contemporary editorial minimalism, tactile packaging cues, origin-led product storytelling, and calm retail navigation.

### Core principles

1. **Balance over spectacle:** Every section should feel composed, warm, and intentional rather than loud or overly decorative.
2. **Origin before ornament:** The collection, source, process, and tasting notes receive the clearest hierarchy.
3. **Ritual as product experience:** Brewing guidance is useful and inviting, not prescriptive.
4. **Warm precision:** Charcoal, warm ivory, roast brown, and aged brass create a premium but human atmosphere.

### Interface improvements

- The cover now introduces MIZAN clearly with a stronger coffee proposition and a direct path to the collection.
- The navigation is sticky, becomes more opaque on scroll, exposes the brand system, and retains a compact mobile menu.
- The page now includes an interactive collection selector with origin, roast, altitude, process, and tasting information.
- A brew ritual section adds repeatable starting points for pour-over and espresso without pretending that ordering is already live.
- A field-notes section gives the brand editorial depth and creates natural future paths for content marketing.
- The waitlist form is implemented as a clear frontend placeholder and uses a toast until a backend or CRM is connected.
- The language toggle remains available in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout handling.

### Visual decisions

- **Summit Charcoal `#1E2224`:** trust and depth.
- **Warm Ivory `#F2EBDD`:** breathing room and editorial reading surfaces.
- **Roast Brown `#4A3025`:** material warmth and sensory detail.
- **Brass Note `#B89152`:** restrained emphasis for rules, seals, numerals, and actions.
- DM Serif Display remains the expressive display face; Manrope carries utility, navigation, and body copy.
- The MIZAN mark is drawn in CSS as a small balance/orbit/bean symbol so the new name never depends on an old APEX wordmark asset.

### Future enhancements

The next practical product step is to connect the collection cards and waitlist to a real commerce or CRM backend. The current frontend deliberately labels these paths as pending so the public site does not imply that checkout or email capture is already active.
