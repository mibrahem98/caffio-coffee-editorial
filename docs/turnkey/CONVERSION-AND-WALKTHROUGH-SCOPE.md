# Conversion Section and Buyer Walkthrough Scope

**Reference date:** 19 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Define the truthful conversion and walkthrough scope for the Caffio/MIZAN buyer-facing release.

## Conversion-section rule

The landing page may combine product benefits with **approved real visitor reflections only**. It must never seed, paraphrase, simulate, or hard-code reviews, ratings, customer counts, purchase claims, testimonials, or star averages. If no approved reflection exists, the section must render an attractive but explicit empty state and provide non-commercial next actions such as collection, product record, comparison, or field notes.

The benefits shown in the section must be product-experience capabilities already present in the application: bilingual RTL/LTR experience, evidence-state product records, record-guided search and comparison, practical brew guidance, and clearly labeled local commerce simulations. The section must not represent payment, shipping, customer service, or fulfillment as live.

## Buyer walkthrough rule

The approved walkthrough is a **90-second horizontal 16:9 screen recording** with no voiceover and a low-volume calm music bed. Its job is to demonstrate actual running routes and interactions—not to advertise hypothetical operation. It must cover the home experience, locale/theme controls, collection/product record, search, comparison, brew guide, field notes, FAQ, favorites, local cart, Society, tracking, profile, and payment-activity empty state.

Any frame containing cart, Society, tracking, profile, or payments must retain the existing local/demo/empty disclosure. The recording must not enter an administrator area, expose account identity, create real payment activity, or imply data collection. A shot plan and a capture log must identify the exact routes and states used.

> The video demonstrates the recorded build at capture time. It is not a guarantee of future uptime, conversion rate, live integration, security certification, or rights transfer.
