# Buyer FAQ, Reflection, and Licensed-Audio Scope

## Conversion-proof rule

The public conversion section may present documented capabilities and **approved** visitor reflections only. It must not display invented names, ratings, quotations, purchase histories, customer counts, or testimonial-like placeholder copy. Until a moderator approves a real reflection, the published proof state remains the existing transparent empty state.

## Buyer FAQ rule

Add a clearly labelled buyer-facing FAQ group alongside the existing coffee guidance. It may explain the included codebase, bilingual/RTL behavior, local-demo boundaries, documented QA, source-governance process, handover preparation, and what a buyer must connect before live commerce. It must not promise ownership transfer, security certification, uptime, live payment, shipping, customer data, or recurring revenue.

## Licensed-audio acceptance rule

Before mixing any uploaded track, record its uploaded filename, format, duration, and the user’s explicit statement that they have the right to use it in this project. Reject unsupported or corrupt files. A web-sourced replacement remains prohibited **unless the user explicitly delegates research, comparison, and selection**, as they later did for this release. In that exception, use the original provider download only, capture the live licence category, provider URL, title, creator, filename, technical probe, and SHA-256, and reject candidates with unresolved Content ID risk.

Mix the accepted instrumental track beneath the visual demo at a low level; retain no voiceover. Render a new 90-second H.264 1280×720 MP4, then publish a new checksum and delivery-status note. The documentation records licence evidence at download time but never represents music rights as transferable outside the provider’s terms.
